# Product limitations

- This is an operator review assistant, not an autonomous safety system.
- Temporal-only events have a high false-positive risk.
- The frozen detector can miss people.
- Suppression rules require explicit operator confirmation and remain auditable.
- Processing may be slower than source video playback.
- The long benchmark measures throughput on a development/sample input only.
- No railway test data was opened for this product.
- Models and restricted datasets are not distributed.
- No alarm output, interlocking integration, train control or safety actuation
  is implemented.
